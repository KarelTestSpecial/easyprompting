Privacy Policy for "Easy Prompting"

Last updated: 2026.10.10 (version 1.1)

This Privacy Policy describes how "Easy Prompting" ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The extension functions entirely on your local device. Your prompt drafts never leave your computer and are never sent to us or any third party. The extension makes no network requests of any kind.

2. Information We Use
The extension uses the `chrome.storage` permission to save your prompt drafts and preferences (such as text size) locally on your own device, so your work is preserved across browser sessions. Nothing is transmitted over the network.

3. Permissions
Easy Prompting requests four permissions, all used locally and only for the features described here:
- `windows` — opens, focuses, moves and closes the pad window. This covers the floating shape of the pad; the default shape is a regular browser tab and needs no special permission.
- `storage` — saves your drafts and preferences locally, as described in section 2.
- `system.display` — read once when a floating window is created, so it can open in a predictable position. It is not used for anything else.
- `contextMenus` — adds a right-click menu to the extension's own toolbar icon only (`contexts: ["action"]`). The menu switches the pad between its two shapes or closes it, and never appears on web pages, tabs or selections.
None of these permissions read the content of the pages you visit, and none of them send any data anywhere.

4. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
